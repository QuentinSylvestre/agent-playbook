# Case Study Archetype: Semantic Premium App Icon

## Archetype

Semantic/iconic premium app icon.

## Transferable lesson

A literal metaphor can work when the metaphor is precise, layered, and rendered with discipline.

## Example qualities

- Core metaphor: lab instrument + signal waveform + transformation.
- Category fit: developer/data/streaming tool.
- Rendering: dark-mode-native neon glass with cyan/magenta signal energy.
- Strength: instantly explains “a lab for streams/signals.”
- Risk: weaker as a long-term standalone brand mark if it depends too heavily on literal flask/wave symbols.

## What to copy

- Use a metaphor system, not a random object.
- Combine object + data behavior when the product transforms information.
- Make rich rendering support the metaphor.
- Keep a high-contrast centered silhouette.

## What not to copy blindly

- Do not make every technical tool neon/cyberpunk.
- Do not use flask/wave imagery unless it fits the product.
- Do not assume a good app icon is automatically a durable brand mark.
