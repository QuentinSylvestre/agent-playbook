# Intake Questions

Ask one at a time by default. Adapt based on the user's answer.

Recommended opening sequence:

1. What is the app/project name?
2. What does it do in one sentence?
3. Who is it for?
4. Where will the identity appear? desktop, web, mobile, tray/menu bar, PWA, docs, public website?
5. Is this personal/internal or public/commercial?
6. Are there existing screenshots, app UI, logos, generated concepts, or references to inspect?
7. Are there privacy/proprietary concerns I should avoid exposing in prompts, examples, filenames, generated assets, or summaries?
8. What emotional tone should it have? Freeform is fine.
9. What should it not feel like?
10. Any colors, metaphors, competitors, or clichés to avoid?

If the user says “skip questions,” state assumptions before proceeding.
