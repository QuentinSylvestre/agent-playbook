## Kiro QA Discipline

Re-read `/qqa` SKILL.md in full before running a verification — context pressure degrades recall of the Surface taxonomy, the Verdict report format, and the Destructive-path guard.

### On the Probe mandate

The Probe mandate is not optional and not satisfiable by happy-path confirmation. A PASS with zero probes is confirmation bias, not verification; a single happy-adjacent probe rarely reaches the state-dependent defects that matter. Do not rationalize skipping the ≥2-probe / at-least-one-state-dependent floor because the change "looks correct" — the floor exists precisely for changes that look correct and are not.

### On the By-design rationalization guard

Re-read `shared/skills/qqa/SKILL.md § By-design rationalization guard` before acting and apply it literally; do not skip it under time pressure.
